# Judge Scorecard — Neon Poster (gta6-cover-art-01)

Eight candidate covers were rendered by the app itself (shot mode, 1920x1080 canvas,
shown at 1280x720) and scored 0-2 per judge criterion, in brief order:
1. The art · 2. Any photo · 3. Fast and easy · 4. Polish.
Criterion 2/3/4 scores rate how well each candidate *shows* that strength.
All candidates are original: our own lettering in Anton / Archivo Black (SIL OFL),
AI-made sample photos, no Rockstar logo, artwork, character or font anywhere.

## Head-to-head (criteria 1-2 decide first)

C1, C2, C5, C7 and C8 all score 4/4 on criteria 1+2. Criterion 4 breaks the tie:
C2, C5 and C8 (2) beat C1 and C7 (1). Among those three, C8 wins criterion 1
head-to-head: the character fills the hero panel the way GTA key art leads with a
character, the yellow Anton title has the highest contrast of the set, and the two
side panels (neon car, palm skyline) complete the Vice City sunset story.

## Winner

**C8 Neon Harbor** — Hero + Duo layout, Sunset Vice grade, Anton title "NEON HARBOR"
in Sun Yellow, subtitle "A SUNSET STORY", photos boulevard / neon-car / palm-sunset.
It is the app's default preset and screenshot `1-cover.jpg`. The other final
screenshots reuse C3 (square, with crop pans fixed) and C2 (portrait) to show the
other post sizes, the second font and a second grade.

Candidate renders live in `candidates/` (outside `app/`, so they are not part of
the dossier).
